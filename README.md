One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist, for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e).

## Entry

> In Quillhaven, snow falls upwards until noon, then thinks better of it — bring a hat, and leave one for the pigeons.

Run with Node 22: `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).

## Craft

Forecast register kept and bent (snow falls upwards till noon), one concrete detail makes Quillhaven feel like a place, smile in the last words. Saturated angles avoided: rain/drizzle, bus on time/late + "and so are you", umbrella, -wick city names, "cloudy with a chance of free croissants", rain falling sideways.
